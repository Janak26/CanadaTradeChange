## An interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze change in Canada's Trade Relationship with the United States and the Rest of the World.

Technology Used: Python, PowerBI, DAX, PostgreSQL
Data Source: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1210017501

The data from Statistics Canada show that Canada's trade with the United States has decreased, whereas Canada has increased its trade with other trading partners between 2024 and 2025.
Even though US still remains Canada's largest trading partner in terms of dollars, the changing trend is clearly visible.

![USvsRoWLineChart](https://github.com/Janak26/CanadaTradeChange/blob/main/images/visualizationPDF_001.jpg)

------------

More data exploration shows the resource wise comparision of trade changes YoY.
Canada decreased exporting majority of its resources to other trading partners instead of its traditional reliance on the United States.

![ResourceWiseExports](https://github.com/Janak26/CanadaTradeChange/blob/main/images/visualizationPDF_002.jpg)

------------

Similarly, Canada decreased its imports from the United States and diversified its imports to cut reliance on a single trading partner.
Both, the import and export changes are ideal for every country or business. Have multiple clients and suppliers to reduce reliance and increase diversification.

![ResourceWiseImports](https://github.com/Janak26/CanadaTradeChange/blob/main/images/visualizationPDF_003.jpg)

------------

A 2024 vs 2025 country and resource wise comparison shows small but reducing reliance on trade with the United states across multiple resources, with countries like United Kingdom and China filling the gaps.
